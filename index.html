Game tutorial

What is the goal?

In this game, you need to make the white ball go up the whole to make it touch the green round

How does I make the ball go up?

On computer, you use your keys to pull or release the ropes, the central ones pull and the other ones release them.
  • A: release the left rope
  • S: pull the left rope
  • K: pull the right rope
  • L: release the right rope

Special controls :o

Change level by clicking Z to go to the previous or N to go to the next
On Phone, you scroll on the canvas to pull or release the ropes, scroll down to pull and up to release.
  • Scroll up at the left: release the left rope
  • Scroll down at the left: pull the left rope
  • Scroll up at the right: pull the right rope
  • Scroll down at the right: release the right rope

Swipe vertically !

Swiping diagonally or horizontally do nothing :3
On your BKpad, you turn the potentiometers to pull or release the ropes, think them as weel!
  • ↻ at the left: release the left rope
  • ↺ at the left: pull the left rope
  • ↻ at the right: pull the right rope
  • ↺ at the right: release the right rope

Special controls :o

You can go to the previous or next level by clicking your 2 buttons under the potentiometers :D
