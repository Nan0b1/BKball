BKball

In this game you will have to make the ball go into the green circle by pulling on the ropes :)
There isn't ropes in your keyboard ? Use the next controls !
Welcome in the editor!
 To add a new circle: Click somewhere on he board and enter the diameter in the popup
To make a green circle click onto a gray circle
To delete a hole click one time on the green circle or 2 times on the gray circle !
