computa~On computer, you use your keys to pull or release the ropes, the central ones pull and the other ones release them.
A~<b>A:</b> release the left rope
S~<b>S:</b> pull the left rope
L~<b>K:</b> pull the right rope
K~<b>L:</b> release the right rope
speControl~Special controls :o
speControls~Change level by clicking <b>Z</b> to go to the previous or <b>N</b> to go to the next
phone~On Phone, you scroll on the canvas to pull or release the ropes, scroll down to pull and up to release.<br>
APhone~<b>Scroll up at the left:</b> release the left rope
SPhone~<b>Scroll down at the left:</b> pull the left rope
LPhone~<b>Scroll up at the right:</b> pull the right rope
KPhone~<b>Scroll down at the right:</b> release the right rope
speControlPhone~Swipe vertically !
speControlsPhone~Swiping diagonally or horizontally do nothing :3
BKpad_~On your <a href="https://github.com/Nan0b1/BKpad"><b>BKpad</b></a>, you turn the potentiometers to pull or release the ropes, think them as weel!<br>
ABK~<b>↻ at the left:</b> release the left rope
SBK~<b>↺ at the left:</b> pull the left rope
KBK~<b>↻ at the right:</b> pull the right rope
LBK~<b>↺ at the right:</b> release the right rope
speControlsBK~You can go to the previous or next level by clicking your 2 buttons under the potentiometers :D
